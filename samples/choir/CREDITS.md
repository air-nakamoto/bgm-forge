# Choir — Freesound 764124

- Original: CRWDSing_U Ooh Vowel Sung Near F 02_ShaneVincent_GSC24_FUMA-AmbeoVR.wav
  by ShangusBurger
- Source: https://freesound.org/people/ShangusBurger/sounds/764124/
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal, verified 2026-09-20 on the source page.
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Description: a medium-sized crowd singing the vowel "u" near concert F, recorded at
  GameSoundCon 2024 with a Sennheiser Ambeo VR microphone. 4-channel B-format (FuMa).
- Processing: only the omnidirectional W channel (the first) is used. Cropped to
  1.45–8.30 s, where the pitch is steady, mono 32 kHz, DC removed, peak normalized to 0.85
  (the original peaks at 0.072), 60 ms attack fade and 550 ms tail fade. Loop points sit an
  integer number of periods apart at zero crossings, so long notes can sustain.
  Signed 16-bit PCM embedded in bank.js.
- Measured at 177.78 Hz (F3 +31 cents), periodicity 0.980. It is a crowd, so individual
  singers spread around that pitch; that spread is the chorus effect and is kept.
  Exact values and the original checksum are in manifest.json.
